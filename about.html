About Vandskar.

Vandskar Advanced Systems is a Swiss company developing advanced communications and artificial intelligence infrastructure for environments where access to reliable information is critical.

Our foundations are in scientific research, advanced algorithms, secure computing and the delivery of complex technical systems. We bring that experience to a new generation of operational technologies.

A foundation in research and engineering.

Our technical background spans artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistical modelling, healthcare computing and secure data infrastructure.

The experience behind Vandskar includes building and delivering algorithms, databases and computational systems used in research and clinical environments where accuracy, reliability and data protection matter.

This work has involved academic institutions, university hospitals and international research collaborations, with scientific contributions published in established peer-reviewed journals.

Experience delivering complex systems.

Data and computation

Experience managing large volumes of sensitive information, developing high-throughput computational pipelines and operating secure data-processing infrastructure across institutional environments.

Algorithms and intelligence

Development of statistical algorithms, machine-learning methods, AI applications, evidence databases and agentic systems for complex analytical and decision-support tasks.

Systems and infrastructure

Engineering experience across software architecture, networking, industrial infrastructure, systems integration and the allocation of computational and operational resources.

Delivery and translation

Experience taking advanced methods beyond research into working software and operational programmes, alongside technical leadership, resource planning, funding and collaboration with institutional partners.

Advanced intelligence. Familiar technology.

Powerful analytical systems have traditionally required specialist software, technical expertise and direct access to computing infrastructure. These requirements can make advanced capabilities difficult to use at the point where decisions are made.

Our direction is to bring those capabilities into the communications equipment and workflows that professionals already use.

Bifrost, our first product, is being developed to connect physical communications systems with organisation-controlled AI models, databases and specialist analytical tools.

An operator should be able to use a familiar radio or communications interface while the underlying system handles speech processing, authorised information access, analytical workflows and delivery of a concise response.

Our engineering approach prioritises secure integration, practical deployment and preserving human responsibility for consequential decisions.

Development and partnerships.

We are advancing our systems through engineering development, research and collaboration with prospective deployment partners. We are also raising capital to support technical validation, product development and adoption.

We welcome discussions with organisations and partners interested in bringing advanced AI capabilities into operational environments.
