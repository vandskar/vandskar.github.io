Data protection

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Swiss data protection law, including the Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Where applicable, we also observe other relevant data protection requirements.

Information we collect

Website access

When visiting this website, technical information may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers. This may include IP addresses, browser information, requested pages, timestamps and connection information.

Communications

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide to respond to your enquiry and manage the related correspondence.

Please do not send sensitive, classified or confidential operational information through ordinary email without first agreeing on an appropriate secure channel.

Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website engagement and improve our content.

Analytics information may include page views, navigation patterns, engagement events, approximate geographic location, referring websites and technical characteristics of browsers and devices.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers. Although analytics reports are generally aggregated, underlying identifiers and technical information may constitute personal data.

Analytics data may be processed by Google and its service providers outside Switzerland, including in the United States.

For further information, consult Google's privacy policy .

Cookie controls

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on .

Where applicable law requires prior consent for non-essential analytics technologies, such consent must be obtained before those technologies are activated.

Hosting and service providers

This website is published using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical connection information to deliver the website, maintain availability and protect their services.

We use external service providers only for purposes connected with operating, maintaining and improving the website and our communications.

Processing by international providers may involve transfers of personal information outside Switzerland. The applicable data protection requirements and transfer safeguards depend on the provider, destination and processing arrangement.

Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.

Correspondence may be retained for the duration of the relevant business relationship or enquiry. Analytics retention is determined by the configured settings of the analytics service and applicable legal requirements.

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about personal data we hold concerning you, seek correction of inaccurate information or request deletion where appropriate.

You may also have rights to object to certain processing activities or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
