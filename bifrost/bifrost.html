Intelligence over radio.

Give your people direct access to your organisation's private knowledge and advanced AI intelligence, wherever they operate. Your organisation's intelligence. Wherever you are.

Organisations hold enormous amounts of valuable knowledge across private databases, operational records, technical documentation and specialist analytical systems. Much of this information is difficult to access outside the organisation's computing environment.

Bifrost connects these resources to people through their existing communications channels, including radio. An operator asks a question and receives information from the organisation's approved intelligence systems, without needing to access the underlying databases or software.

In a private deployment, the source data and AI infrastructure remain within the organisation's controlled environment. The operator receives the relevant response rather than carrying copies of sensitive databases or intelligence systems into the field.

Your data. Your AI. Your control.

Bifrost gives organisations a common communications interface to their selected knowledge and intelligence resources, while allowing the underlying technology to be chosen according to operational requirements.

Keep critical knowledge on site

Your private databases, operational procedures, technical records and specialist knowledge can remain inside your own infrastructure. Authorised personnel access the information they need without requiring the complete source material to be distributed to their devices.

Choose the intelligence

Use privately hosted open-weight AI models, approved commercial providers or a combination of systems. Select the most appropriate intelligence for each task according to capability, performance, cost and information sensitivity.

Fully private deployments can keep both data and AI processing within organisational infrastructure. Where commercial providers are used, external processing and permitted information transfers are governed by the chosen deployment configuration.

Preserve organisational standards

Leadership determines which knowledge sources are authoritative, how information is organised, who may access it and which intelligence services support particular activities.

Bifrost provides a consistent route to organisation-approved information, helping personnel work from established procedures, policies and knowledge rather than disconnected or individually interpreted sources.

Direct answers. Fewer delays.

In conventional operations, a field operator may need to contact a colleague, wait for an analyst to locate information and rely on several people to relay the result. Each hand-off takes time and creates opportunities for information to be lost, simplified or misunderstood.

Bifrost gives authorised personnel a direct communications route to approved knowledge and analytical resources. Routine questions can be answered using the organisation's intelligence infrastructure without requiring an analyst to manually retrieve and relay every answer.

Information can be delivered in a format suited to the operator and the situation, including concise spoken responses through radio. The operator remains focused on the task, using familiar equipment.

Direct access reduces unnecessary communication layers while preserving access to human expertise whenever judgement, verification or escalation is required.

Human expertise remains in command.

Bifrost brings advanced analytical capability into existing teams and communications networks. AI supports the people involved, while responsibility and authority remain with the organisation's designated personnel.

Intelligence for every authorised operator

Personnel can draw on extensive organisational knowledge and advanced AI analysis without requiring specialist computing skills or direct access to complex technical systems.

Experts remain connected

Analysts, specialists and supervisors can remain part of the workflow. Organisations can establish when information is provided directly and when requests require human review, confirmation or escalation.

Leadership defines the rules

Information access, approved knowledge sources, operational procedures and decision responsibilities follow the organisation's established governance. Bifrost supports a shared operational understanding across teams rather than introducing an independent source of authority.

The result is a team equipped with substantially greater analytical reach, supported by the organisation's own intelligence and specialists.

Intelligence stays protected. People stay connected.

Sensitive information does not need to travel into the field as complete databases, local document collections or installed AI systems. Bifrost separates access to intelligence from physical possession of the underlying resources.

Organisations can retain control of their data, computing infrastructure and approved information services while providing field users with task-specific responses.

Security is determined by the complete deployment, including the communications network, authentication, access permissions, data processing and transmission protections. Compatible protected communications channels can be used where confidentiality is required.

The objective is to make powerful organisational intelligence accessible without unnecessarily distributing the systems and information on which it depends.
