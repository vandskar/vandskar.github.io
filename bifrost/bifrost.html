Bifrost.
Intelligence over radio.

Bifrost is a communications system being developed to connect people in the field with their organisation's AI models, critical data and specialist knowledge. It bridges physical communications equipment and controlled digital intelligence systems, allowing operators to request information and receive spoken responses through familiar communications channels.

Explore the system ↗

From radio to intelligence.

Conventional radio connects people. Bifrost is designed to extend that connection to an organisation's analytical infrastructure.

Radio traffic enters a communications interface, where speech can be converted into structured requests and routed to approved intelligence resources. Responses can then be converted into speech and returned through the communications channel.

The operator does not need to interact directly with an AI application, database or computer interface.

A communications system, not a chatbot.

Radio integration

Interface with compatible communications equipment to receive voice transmissions and manage the return audio path, including push-to-talk signalling where supported.

Speech processing

Convert spoken requests into text, process them through an approved intelligence workflow and synthesise concise spoken responses.

Policy and routing

Provide a control layer for selecting authorised services, applying operational rules and directing requests to appropriate models, tools or specialist workflows.

Secure systems integration

Connect to organisation-approved infrastructure through controlled interfaces. The security architecture, access controls and data-processing boundaries are determined by the deployment environment.

Your intelligence. Your infrastructure.

Bifrost is intended to operate with the resources an organisation already uses, including private AI models, internal databases, technical documentation, analytical services and approved external providers.

Its architecture is being designed to accommodate different processing locations, network boundaries and organisational requirements. Sensitive information need not be carried directly by the field operator when it can instead be accessed through an authorised communications workflow.

Where necessary, responses can be subject to human review, approval or escalation before delivery.

Designed for operational environments.

Defence and security. Field personnel accessing approved operational knowledge, technical guidance and decision-support resources through existing communications workflows.

Healthcare. Clinical teams requesting information from hospital-approved knowledge systems without leaving their immediate work.

Industry and aviation. Operators consulting specialist procedures, equipment information and technical systems through voice-based interfaces.

Bifrost provides information to support human decisions. Operational responsibility remains with the people and organisations using the system.

Built around the operational requirement.

Bifrost is under active development. Its intended architecture combines physical communications, speech processing and organisation-controlled intelligence access. Specific capabilities depend on validated hardware integrations, infrastructure and deployment requirements.
